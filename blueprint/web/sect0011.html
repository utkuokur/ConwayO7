4.4 The generator action on orbit words

Definition 76 Vertex action of a generator
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 67

Lean declarations

  • ConwayO7.Encoder.permVertex

Each of the \(101\) relabelling generators of Definition 67 is given by an explicit map on the vertex set \(\{ 0,\dots ,98\} \); this vertex map induces the generator’s action on pair orbits and hence on words.

Computed Fact 77 Compatibility with the orbit structure
✓
#

Uses

  • Definition 67
  • Definition 76
  • Lemma 33

Lean declarations

For each of the \(101\) generators \(\rho \) and every pair of distinct vertices \(u,v\), the orbit index assigned by \(\rho \) to the orbit of \(\{ u,v\} \) is the orbit of \(\{ \rho (u),\rho (v)\} \): the action on orbit words is induced by the action on vertices. (Checked by computation over all \(101 \cdot \binom {99}{2}\) instances.)

Lemma 79 One generator step preserves strong regularity
#

Uses

  • Proposition 37
  • Computed Fact 77
  • Computed Fact 78
  • Lemma 29

Lean declarations

  • ConwayO7.Encoder.step_srg

If \(G_\omega \) is an \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\) and \(\rho \) is one of the \(101\) generators, then \(G_{\rho \cdot \omega }\) is again an \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\).

Proof sketch ▶

By Fact 78 the vertex map of \(\rho \) is a bijection of the vertex set; by Fact 77 it defines a graph isomorphism \(G_{\rho \cdot \omega } \cong G_\omega \). Conclude by Lemma 29.

Lemma 80 One generator step preserves the normalisation
#

Uses

  • Definition 67
  • Definition 40
  • Definition 76

Lean declarations

  • ConwayO7.Encoder.step_sym1
  • ConwayO7.Encoder.lexPerms_sym1

Each of the \(101\) generators permutes the fourteen orbits of pairs \(\{ 0, 1+7i\} \) among themselves and preserves the predicate “\(i {\lt} 2\)” on their indices; consequently, if the word \(\omega \) satisfies the normalisation condition (3) of Definition 40, so does \(\rho \cdot \omega \).

Proof sketch ▶

The generators normalise the stabiliser structure at the fixed vertex: each sends a block either to another block wholesale, and blocks \(0,1\) to blocks \(0,1\). The corresponding permutation of the fourteen distinguished orbit indices, together with the invariance of “\(i{\lt}2\)”, is checked by computation for each generator; the preservation of condition (3) follows by rewriting each prescribed bit of \(\rho \cdot \omega \) as a prescribed bit of \(\omega \).

Lemma 82 Reachability preserves the invariants
#

Uses

  • Definition 81
  • Lemma 79
  • Lemma 80

Lean declarations

If \(G_\omega \) is an \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\), then so is \(G_{\omega '}\) for every \(\omega ' \in \mathcal{R}(\omega )\); and if \(\omega \) satisfies the normalisation condition, so does every \(\omega ' \in \mathcal{R}(\omega )\).

Proof sketch ▶

Induction along the chain of generator steps, using Lemmas 79 and 80 at each step.
