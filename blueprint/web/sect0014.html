Every \(\sigma _0\)-invariant \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\) admits an isomorphic copy whose orbit word \(\omega \) satisfies conditions (3)–(4) of Definition 40: the normalisation of Lemma 75 and the lex-leader invariant \(\omega \preceq \rho \cdot \omega \) for each of the \(101\) generators \(\rho \).

Proof sketch ▶

Normalise first: by Lemma 75 pass to a word \(\omega _0\) that is strongly regular and normalised. Among the words reachable from \(\omega _0\) (Definition 81), choose one of minimal value, \(\omega _{\min }\) (Lemma 85); by Lemma 82 it is still strongly regular and normalised. For each generator \(\rho \) the word \(\rho \cdot \omega _{\min }\) is again reachable, so \(\mathrm{val}(\omega _{\min }) \le \mathrm{val}(\rho \cdot \omega _{\min })\), and by Lemma 84 value order refines lexicographic order: \(\omega _{\min } \preceq \rho \cdot \omega _{\min }\). Translating the word-level comparison into the literal-level comparison of Definition 86 gives the lex-leader invariant.

Every valuation of the orbit block satisfying the master invariant extends, over the auxiliary variables, to a valuation satisfying every clause of the generated formula \(\Phi \).

Proof sketch ▶

The four sections are emitted in sequence, each consuming one conjunct of the master invariant and preserving the rest (each section’s auxiliary variables are fresh, so the other conjuncts, which depend only on the orbit block, are untouched). The counting sections extend by Proposition 62, the comparison section by Proposition 89; composing the four extension steps yields a satisfying valuation of the whole formula. That the abstractly described pipeline emits exactly the clauses of \(\Phi \) is checked by computation.

Lemma 93 Faithfulness from completeness of minimality

Assume completeness of minimality (Theorem 90). If a \(\sigma _0\)-invariant \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\) exists, then the distributed formula is satisfiable.

Proof sketch ▶

An invariant graph descends to an orbit word \(\omega \) (Proposition 37) whose associated graph is strongly regular by transport (Lemma 29). Completeness of minimality replaces \(\omega \) by a companion word satisfying strong regularity, the normalisation, and the lex-leader invariant; the arithmetic conditions (1)–(2) hold for the orbit word of any strongly regular graph, so the companion satisfies the master invariant. Proposition 92 extends it to a satisfying valuation of the generated formula, which equals the distributed file by the byte identity (Fact 43).
