2.1 Fixed points of prime-order permutations

We begin with elementary counting lemmas about permutations \(\pi \) with \(\pi ^p = 1\) for a prime \(p\). Throughout, \(\operatorname {Fix}\pi \) denotes the set of fixed points and \(\operatorname {supp}\pi \) its complement, the support.

  • ConwayO7.card_support_of_pow_prime_eq_one

Let \(\pi \) be a permutation of a finite type with \(\pi ^p = 1\) for a prime \(p\). Then \(|\operatorname {supp}\pi |\) is a multiple of \(p\).

source: ConwayO7/CycleType.lean

Proof ▶

Either \(\pi = 1\), with empty support, or \(\operatorname {ord}\pi = p\); in the latter case the cycle type of \(\pi \) is a multiset of copies of \(p\), and the support is the disjoint union of the cycles.

  • ConwayO7.card_filter_fixed_modEq

Let \(\pi \) be a permutation with \(\pi ^p = 1\), \(p\) prime, and let \(s\) be a finite \(\pi \)-invariant set. Then

\[ |\{ a \in s \mid \pi a = a\} | \equiv |s| \pmod p . \]

source: ConwayO7/CycleType.lean

Proof ▶

Since \(\pi \) is injective and \(s\) is finite, \(\pi (s) \subseteq s\) upgrades to \(\pi (s) = s\), so \(\pi \) restricts to a permutation \(\pi '\) of \(s\) with \((\pi ')^p = 1\). By Lemmas 6 and 7 applied to \(\pi '\), the fixed points of \(\pi \) inside \(s\) number \(|s| - pm\) for some \(m\). (We will apply this with \(p = 7\) to the vertex set and to a neighbourhood, and with \(p = 2\) to a matching involution.)

  • ConwayO7.cycleType_eq_replicate_of_fixed

Let \(\pi \ne 1\) be a permutation of a finite type of cardinality \(f + pm\) with \(\pi ^p = 1\), \(p\) prime, and exactly \(f\) fixed points. Then the cycle type of \(\pi \) is \([p^m]\).

source: ConwayO7/Canonical.lean

Proof ▶

Since \(\pi \ne 1\) and \(\pi ^p = 1\), the order of \(\pi \) is exactly \(p\), so its cycle type is \([p^n]\) for some \(n \ge 1\). By Lemma 7 the support has \(pm\) elements, and the cycle lengths sum to the support size, so \(pn = pm\) and \(n = m\).
