2 From an order-seven automorphism to a canonical one

This chapter carries out the group-theoretic reduction: the statement “\(7 \nmid |\operatorname {Aut}\Gamma |\) for every \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\) \(\Gamma \)” is reduced to a single concrete assertion about one explicit permutation \(\sigma _0\) of \(\{ 0,\dots ,98\} \), namely that no \(\sigma _0\)-invariant \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\) exists (Theorem 25). The mathematical content follows Cesarz–Woldar: an order-seven automorphism of an \(\mathrm{srg}(99,14,1,2)\) fixes exactly one vertex, hence has cycle type \([1,7^{14}]\), hence is conjugate in the symmetric group to the canonical block rotation \(\sigma _0\).
